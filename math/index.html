Weight

Pounds → kg
halve, then −10%

200 lb → 100 → 90 kg · actual 91
kg → pounds
double, then +10%

Ounces → grams
× 30

Trap
US short ton = 907 kg, not 1000 kg. A metric tonne is ~10% heavier. Matters for freight.
