    Yard Master

    Campaign is the packing puzzle: the score is the volume of the box around your stack, so sprawl and air both cost you. Tower is the other way round — no bay, no manifest, just gravity and how high you dare go.

    WebGL · Three.js · no build step

    Line Missions

    How to play

    The loop

    1. Cargo arrives in a fixed order. The unit at the head of the queue is the one you are placing.
    2. Move the pointer over the bay — the ghost shows where it lands. Gravity takes it to the top of whatever is underneath.
    3. Green means legal, red means a regulation says no, and the HUD names which one.
    4. Click to lock it in. R rotates, Z undoes, Esc opens the menu.
    5. When the manifest is empty the yard is measured.

    Scoring

    The envelope is the smallest box containing every slot you used — width × depth × height taken from the outermost coordinates. A stray container parked away from the pile widens the box on every tier, so it costs far more than the slot it sits in.

    Par is a perfect zero-waste pack of the same manifest. Gold is within 10% of par, silver 30%, bronze 60% — some missions loosen that where a rule makes a clean tiling genuinely hard.

    A few missions run the other way: the goal is the biggest envelope you can make, not the smallest. Two boxes pushed together barely widen the box around them; the same two dragged to opposite corners of the bay, one on the floor and one stacked high, multiply it. Gold there means filling most of the bay's own volume, not staying close to par.

    Cargo

      Regulations

        Tower

        A separate game with the same containers. There is no grid holding anything up — every unit has mass, friction and a centre of gravity, and a stack stands only because it balances. Height counts once the tower stops moving; one collapse ends the run and the best is kept.

        Placing to within a fingertip is not good enough for a tall stack, so the toolbar carries an X/Y/Z console. Aim roughly, then step the container over in whole slots, raise the height it is released from, and commit with Drop. On a keyboard that is the arrow keys, Shift with them for height, and Space to let go. On a phone the first tap aims and a second tap on the same spot drops, which leaves room to nudge in between.

        Sandbox

        No grid, no rules, no score. Spawn anything anywhere, unload the train, change the weather, X-ray the boxes.

        Two machines, one keyboard — pick 🚜 Stacker or 🏗️ Crane and it takes the controls. WASD drives, Q/E works the boom or the hoist, and Space grabs or releases. The gantry crane spans the rail tracks and can lift a container straight off a flatcar.

        Contracts turns the sandbox into a job board: timed delivery orders with a payout, a reputation score and upgrades to buy. Move cargo into the marked zone before the clock runs out — cargo already parked there does not count.
