    Yard missions

    How to play

    • Point and click to drop the box on the hook. The green outline is where it lands; red means the yard refuses it, and the reason is on the banner.
    • R rotates 90° about the vertical (right-click does too). Z undoes.
    • Arrow keys / WASD nudge the cursor a cell at a time; Space or Enter drops.
    • The score is the bounding box — the smallest cuboid containing everything you placed, in cubic metres. Holes inside it still count, so pack tight in all three directions.
    • Hard rules stop you: nothing above the tier ceiling, nothing on a tank frame, and enough of a box's underside has to be carried.
    • Soft rules cost volume at the audit: a reefer with no plug access, a flagged box buried under another.
    • Stacker mode (sandbox only) gives you the reach stacker: WASD to drive, Q/E for the boom, Space to lift and set down.
    • Gantry mode (sandbox only) drives the rail-mounted crane instead: W/S runs the gantry along its rails, A/D tracks the trolley across the bridge, Q/E works the hoist, Space lifts and sets down. The cyan beam is where the spreader is over.
