How it works. Each letter is always the same step of the scale, so names that share letters share phrases. Vowels are held, consonants move on, a doubled letter is one long note, and an accent like the ring on å adds a grace note. The name as a whole picks the key, the mode, the tempo, the chords under the tune and the instrument it is played on, so no two names sound alike.
