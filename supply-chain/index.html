How to play

Your ⭐ HQ places orders for goods, and new 🏢 customer DCs will start ordering too as you play. Each factory turns two ingredients into its product — the bakery 🍞 needs wheat and water, the smelter 🔩 turns ore and coal into steel for the car factory 🚗. Some chains run deeper and share ingredients: the robot factory 🤖 needs a circuit board 🔌 (itself made from wire 🧵 and electronics 💾) plus the same steel your cars use. Your trucks do the hauling — but only where roads exist.

  • Build roads: tap a node, then tap another. Crossing the river pops up a choice: a pricier bridge (fast) or a cheaper ferry (slower boat crossing).
  • Fill orders: connect each ingredient's supplier to the factory, and the factory to the ordering HQ/DC. Dispatch is automatic.
  • Earn & grow: payouts fund more roads, trucks, factories and upgrades. New sites appear as you deliver.
  • Credit line: you can spend into the red down to −$1,500, but debt bleeds interest every minute (rate set by difficulty — 15%/min on Normal). If interest drags you below the limit, a default countdown starts — recover in time or the bank forecloses and the run ends. Sandbox never forecloses.
  • Research: in the Shop panel, invest upfront and wait it out to unlock upgrades like manual site placement and a bigger credit line.
  • Truck yards: HQ is your first yard. Build more (Shop panel) to station trucks closer to distant routes — idle trucks head home to their yard, so they stay ready near their own region.
  • Junctions: after the Road Junctions research (cheap, no prerequisites), place one (Shop panel) anywhere on land to fork, merge or reroute roads through a point that isn't a supplier, factory or city — no supply or demand of its own, just a waypoint for shaping the network.
  • Supplier stock: suppliers hold a finite stock that refills over time — trucks wait at a dry supplier. Upgrade mode (⬆️, bottom right): tap a supplier twice to raise its stock cap and refill rate.
  • Highways: after the Asphalt Paving research, use Upgrade mode on a road to pave it — trucks drive 60% faster there.
  • Congestion: a difficulty trait (on for Normal/Hard, off for Easy/Sandbox) — a busy road glows warmer and slows down past a few trucks, so build parallel routes instead of one mega-highway.
  • Fast-forward: the 1×/2×/4× toggle under the HUD speeds up the whole sim — orders, crafting, trucks, interest, everything.
  • Contracts: occasionally a bulk-order card offers a locked-in premium rate — Accept or Decline before the timer runs out. An accepted contract is a big order with its own deadline; miss it and you pay a penalty on top of the failure, not just a missed order.
  • Move around: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Tap a road twice to demolish it.
  • Inspect mode (🔍, bottom right): hover (or tap on mobile) a node to see its connections — a factory's needed ingredients, a supplier's factories, or a city's open orders — with the relevant roads lit up. Tap again to dismiss.
  • Stats & Achievements (☰ menu): deliveries by product, a money-over-time chart, your busiest road, and milestone badges for things like your first bridge or a 10-truck fleet.
