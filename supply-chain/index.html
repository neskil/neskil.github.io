Supply Chain Tycoon

Cities place orders for products. Factories combine two raw materials into one product. Your trucks do the hauling — but only where roads exist.

  • Build roads: tap a node, then tap another. Crossing the river costs bridge money.
  • Fill orders: connect a supplier of each ingredient to a factory, and the factory to the ordering city. Dispatch is automatic.
  • Earn & grow: payouts fund more roads, trucks, factories and upgrades. New sites appear as you deliver.
  • Move around: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Tap a road twice to demolish it.
