Hole lab

Every drawn hole of a card, measured the way README.md → "The hole lab" asks for: every line and weight from the tee out of every club in the hole's bag (at four moments of the slowest beat on a hole that moves), how many are aces and where, whether a shot aimed straight at the flag drops, and the caddie's card. A coarse sweep here — 1° and 25 weights — spread over the machine's cores; &fine=1 asks for the half-degree one tools/hole-lab.mjs prints, and &quick=1 for the plan and the straight shots alone.
