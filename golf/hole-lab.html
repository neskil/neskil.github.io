Hole lab

Every hole of a card, measured the way the design notes in README.md → "Designing a hole" ask for. Aces is the share of every angle and weight from the tee (and every few moments on a hole with a clock) that drops. Caddie is the fewest strokes the bot's search finds — what par is. Club player plays the caddie's plan with a human's hands, a degree and a half of aim and an eighth of the weight either way, twenty rounds (&rounds= for more): the average and the median, and how often a round goes in the water. It plays the caddie's line, the boldest one, so it is a player who goes for it. The verdict reads the ace share against the targets in the notes. Slow on purpose — several seconds a hole.
